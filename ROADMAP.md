# Upfront: roadmap

**Long-term goal:** become the place where on-chain activity turns into income, and income turns into capital.

**Start:** one product on one chain: pools that pay their owners, with earnings available upfront.

---

## Phase 1: Launch (hackathon → testnet)
- Pools that pay owner, app, referrer and protocol in USDG, with honest total fees.
- Advances from a lender vault, repaid automatically from future fees.
- Website and app per `DESIGN.md`.
- **Done when:** a real pool on testnet goes through launch → trades → offer → repaid, on camera.

## Phase 2: Mainnet (Robinhood Chain)
- Audit and bug bounty (`SECURITY.md`).
- Pools first; advances switched on with small caps.
- Public repayment record per owner.
- Hook listed in the Uniswap hooklist, with an honest description.
- **Tracked:**
  - pools launched,
  - USDG paid to owners,
  - advances funded,
  - repayment rate,
  - lender returns.

## Phase 3: Distribution
- **SDK and API** so any app, bot or launchpad can launch Upfront pools and show offers inside its own product.
- **Referral links:** anyone can share a link that pays them on every trade it sends.
- **Embeddable earnings widget** for creator pages.

## Phase 4: Capital
- **Curated lender vaults:** risk curators run vaults with their own rules (curators already operate on Robinhood Chain).
- **Larger advances** backed by optional liquidity locks.
- **Owner credit history:** an on-chain record that makes the next advance cheaper.
- **Tradable earnings notes:** owners can sell a fixed slice of future fees to many investors.

## Phase 5: Beyond one chain
- Arbitrum One and other Arbitrum chains, using the same contracts and the same rules.
- **Earnings from outside Upfront pools** (other on-chain fee contracts), where repayment can be enforced on-chain.

## What we won't do
- **No token launch as the point of the product.** Revenue comes from fees, not emissions.
- **No hidden fees, ever.** That's the brand.
- **No upgradeable contracts that can change live pools.**
- **No growth through fake numbers.** Every metric we publish is on-chain.
