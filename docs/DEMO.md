# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

Every step is real testnet activity (BUILD_SPEC §15). Record the app at `/app` and the site at `/`.

| Time | Screen | Say | Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Website `/` | "About three quarters of Uniswap v4 trades here go through hooks. The biggest shows 0% and takes about 1%. Apps earn real money but can't raise any." | Scroll the hero, the live strip, then "No hidden cut". |
| 0:20 | `/app/launch` | "Launch a pool. The fee is shown to every trader." | Pick a stock token, set the fee, drag the split bar, add funds, Launch pool. |
| 0:50 | Pool page | "Every trade pays the owner, the app and the referrer, in USDG." | Run swaps on the pool; watch Earned and the chart move. Claim. |
| 1:20 | Pool page, Upfront card | "After a track record, the pool gets an offer from its own history." | Accept the offer; USDG arrives. |
| 2:00 | Pool page | "Each new trade repays it. Ownership can't move while it's open." | More swaps; the bar fills and turns green. |
| 2:30 | `/app/lend` | "Lenders earn from advances that repay themselves." | Show the deposit and the active advance. |
| 2:50 | Website | "Upfront: every trade in your pool pays you, and you can get those earnings upfront." | End on the final call. |

Start trading the demo pool at least a day before recording: an offer needs a track record (testnet: a few days).
